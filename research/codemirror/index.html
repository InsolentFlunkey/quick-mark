Markdown input proof of concept

Press Escape, then Tab to leave the editor. Press F8 to move to the next diagnostic.

Required manual checks

  1. Click, Shift-click, and drag line numbers in both directions. Drag beyond both vertical edges.
  2. Verify blank, wrapped, and final unterminated lines select as whole logical lines.
  3. Type Unicode and use an IME; switch documents and round-trip the transfer.
  4. Apply diagnostics, hover a marked range, press F8, and open keyboard-accessible details.
  5. Press Escape then Tab to leave the editor; return and verify Tab indents again.
